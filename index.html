What can the Polytopia Planner do?

A free fan tool for The Battle of Polytopia. Load a screenshot of your game and plan your next turn right on top of it – on your phone or computer.

  • Damage calculator – place your and enemy units, see damage, retaliation and kills, and get the best attack order.
  • Star planner – tap a tile to see what you can build there and what it costs; plan units, techs and your budget for next turn.
  • Market optimizer – finds the layout of Windmill, Forge, Sawmill and Market with the most stars per turn.
  • Draw & notes – arrows, marks and numbered moves, also by voice.

How is damage calculated in Polytopia?

Attack force = attack × HP / max HP, defense force = defense × HP / max HP × defense bonus (×1.5 on forest, mountain or in a city, ×4 behind a City Wall). Damage = attack force / (attack force + defense force) × attack × 4.5, rounded. A surviving defender in range strikes back the same way with its defense.

How do I get the most stars from a Market?

A Market earns stars per turn equal to the levels of the Windmills, Forges and Sawmills next to it (up to 8). Their levels come from the Farms, Mines and Lumber Huts around them. The Market tool tries every layout and shows the best one.
