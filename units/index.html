FAQ

How many units are there in Polytopia?

The planner knows all 55 current unit types: the standard land units, ships (Raft, Scout, Rammer, Bomber, Juggernaut, Pirate, Dinghy), super units and the special units of Aquarion, ∑∫ỹriȱŋ, Polaris and Cymanti.

Which tribes have different units?

Only the four special tribes: Aquarion (amphibious mermaid units, Shark, Puffer, Jelly, Crab), ∑∫ỹriȱŋ (Polytaur and the Dragon), Polaris (Ice Archer, Mooni, Battle Sled, Ice Fortress, Gaami) and Cymanti (Hexapod, Phychi, Kiton, Doomux, Centipede …). All other tribes share the standard units.

How do ships get their HP?

Raft, Scout, Rammer and Bomber have the HP of the unit they carry – a Swordsman on a Bomber has 15 HP (20 as a veteran).

What does Static mean?

Static units can’t become veterans – ships, super units, Daggers, Pirates and other units that aren’t trained in a city.
