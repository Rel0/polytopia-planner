FAQ

What does the Polytopia tech tree look like?

There are five tier 1 techs – Climbing, Fishing, Hunting, Organization and Riding. Each one leads to two tier 2 techs, and each tier 2 tech to one tier 3 tech: 25 technologies in total.

How much does a technology cost in Polytopia?

Tech cost = tier × number of cities + 4 stars. Tier 1 techs (e.g. Riding, Fishing) are cheapest, tier 3 techs (e.g. Chivalry, Mathematics) the most expensive.

Why do techs get more expensive?

Every city you own increases the price of every tech by its tier: with 5 cities a tier 3 tech costs 3 × 5 + 4 = 19 stars instead of 7.

Does Philosophy make techs cheaper?

Yes – after Philosophy (Literacy) all techs cost about one third less. The star planner applies it automatically when you mark Philosophy as researched.
