Polytopia damage formula

The calculator uses the community-verified combat formula from the Polytopia wiki:

attackForce  = attack  × attackerHP / attackerMaxHP
defenseForce = defense × defenderHP / defenderMaxHP × defenseBonus
total        = attackForce + defenseForce
damage       = round(attackForce  / total × attack  × 4.5)
retaliation  = round(defenseForce / total × defense × 4.5)   (only if the defender survives and is in range)

A veteran has +5 max HP. Ships (Raft, Scout, Rammer, Bomber) use the HP of the unit they carry.

FAQ

How is damage calculated in Polytopia?

attackForce = attack × HP / max HP; defenseForce = defense × HP / max HP × defense bonus. Damage = round(attackForce / (attackForce + defenseForce) × attack × 4.5). If the defender survives and the attacker is within its range, it strikes back with round(defenseForce / total × defense × 4.5).

How does splash damage work in Polytopia?

Units with Splash (Bomber, Fire Dragon) also hit every enemy next to the target. Each of them takes half the damage a normal hit would deal to it, without rounding – an odd damage leaves the unit at x.5 HP. The Exida’s splash only poisons the neighbours.

How much damage does an explosion do?

Exploding units (Boomchi, Doomux, Segment, Insect Egg) deal half of their normal damage to every adjacent enemy and poison them. The exploding unit dies.

What does poison do in Polytopia?

Poison halves the defense bonus of the unit (×1 becomes 0.5, ×1.5 becomes 0.7, a city wall ×4 becomes 2), so it takes more damage. It also prevents healing until the unit recovers.

Does a unit in a forest always get a defense bonus?

No. The ×1.5 bonus on Forest needs Archery, on Mountain Climbing and on Water / ocean Aquatism – for the tribe of the defending unit. Without that tech the unit fights with ×1. In the calculator above, tap the defender and tick or untick “Has Archery” (or Climbing / Aquatism) to compare both results.

Which units get the city defense bonus?

Only units with the Fortify skill (e.g. Warrior, Archer, Defender, Rider, Knight). A Giant, Swordsman or Catapult gets no bonus in a city, but still gets terrain bonuses.

How does the calculator find the best attack order?

Every attacker attacks once. The calculator tries how to split your units over the enemies and in which order they attack – splash first if it weakens the neighbours, the next unit on another enemy if a target is already dead. “Sort by” sets what counts most: most kills, most damage, no own losses or fewest attacks. “Custom” uses only the attacks you choose, in your order.
